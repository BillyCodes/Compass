OUR OBJECTIVE

Why Compass exists

This project aims to prepare Jackson State University students for industry by mapping their coursework and skills to real career requirements and surfacing direct insight from working professionals on what it takes to succeed after graduation. In doing so, it seeks to close the divide between what academic curricula teach and what employers expect of new hires.

RESEARCH QUESTIONS

What we're investigating

  1. What causes the divide between academia and industry?
  2. What motivates companies to invest in Computer Science education and student development?
  3. How do students' skills compare to current industry requirements?
  4. What incentivizes graduates and professionals to give back to their alma mater?
