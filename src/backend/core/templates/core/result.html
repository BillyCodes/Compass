How this score works

For each scored skill, credit is your reported level divided by the required level, capped at 100%. Credits are weighted by importance and averaged. Unreported skills earn zero credit.

Mandatory skills are evaluated separately so a high score can't hide a critical gap. A missing mandatory input leaves their status undetermined unless a known mandatory gap already exists. This score describes skill alignment. It isn't a hiring prediction.
