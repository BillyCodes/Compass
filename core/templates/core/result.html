How this score works

Each required skill earns credit by how close your level is: meets it = 100%, one level short = 60%, two short = 30%, three or more = 10%, missing = 0%. Credits are weighted by importance and averaged.

Mandatory skills are counted separately so a high score can't hide a critical gap. This score describes skill alignment. It isn't a hiring prediction.
